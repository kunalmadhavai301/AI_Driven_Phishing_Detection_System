THREAT ANALYSIS & CASE STUDIES

High-Profile Phishing Data Breaches

Real-world case scenarios demonstrating how spear phishing, domain spoofing, and credential harvesting led to multi-million dollar enterprise breaches.

SPEAR PHISHING & SPOOFING

John Podesta Campaign Breach

In November 2016, attackers impersonated Google security with a fake password reset URL. The compromised email link allowed threat actors to harvest credentials and leak over 50,000 internal communications via WikiLeaks.

EMPLOYEE CREDENTIAL HARVESTING

BenefitMall Payroll Compromise

Employee email credentials exposed through a phishing attack resulted in unauthorized access to payroll and HR databases, exposing personal names, bank routing numbers, and birth dates across 200,000 businesses.

APT & SPEAR-PHISHING RECONNAISSANCE

Sony Pictures Entertainment Attack

State-sponsored attackers utilized spear-phishing emails infected with covert reconnaissance malware to gain network access, exfiltrate confidential files, and cause over $100M in infrastructure damages.

HEALTHCARE PATIENT DATA EXFILTRATION

Methodist Hospitals Data Breach

A targeted email phishing campaign compromised employee mailboxes, exposing sensitive medical records, Social Security numbers, and financial details of over 68,000 patients.

BUSINESS EMAIL COMPROMISE (BEC)

UW-Parkside Wire Fraud Attack

Attackers spoofed vendor correspondence and tricked administrative staff into altering bank routing details, resulting in $315,000 in direct fraudulent bank transfers before detection.
