Frequently Asked Questions

Understand phishing attack vectors and preventative measures.

What is a Phishing Attack?
Phishing is a cyber attack where fraudsters pose as legitimate entities (banks, email providers, services) to trick victims into providing sensitive data like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identity information.
What are the major types of Phishing Attacks?
Major types include Deceptive Phishing (impersonating known services), Spear Phishing (targeted attack on specific individuals), Smishing (SMS phishing), and Domain Spoofing (forging domain names).
How does PhishDetector classify URLs?
PhishDetector extracts 30 distinct feature indicators from the URL structure, WHOIS data, HTML DOM, and SSL metadata, feeding them into a trained Machine Learning model to compute a real-time safety verdict.
